Version 1.1, 2026-10-31.

Version 1.0 was first published on 2026-10-30. This version adds the purpose of the agreement and explains AI-assisted work. It does not treat earlier submissions as consent.

Thank you for your interest in Kindi. This agreement explains what happens when you send a contribution to Kindi.
A contribution is code, documentation, benchmark data, a bug fix or any other material that you send to us for
Kindi.

## Why this agreement exists

I want to keep the right to offer Kindi under a separate commercial license. If I accept work from another
author, I need their permission to include that work under both licenses. This agreement gives that permission
without taking the author's copyright.

Kindi remains open source under AGPL-3.0. Individuals and businesses can use it, including for commercial work,
under the terms of that license. A separate commercial license is an option for users who need other terms.

You do not need to sign this agreement to use Kindi, report a bug, ask a question or discuss an idea. Sending a
report or a pull request does not by itself mean that you have agreed. We ask for explicit agreement before we
include your submitted material under this agreement. We can discuss separate permission for a report or quote.
Credit for a report and acceptance of code are separate matters.
